# Design System

## Visual Language
The environment is DARK and PREMIUM. It uses editorial typography, glass-like panels, depth, and perspective. Do NOT use glassmorphism everywhere—use it selectively.

## Color System
- **Primary background:** Deep, premium dark hues.
- **Secondary background:** Slightly elevated dark tones for contrast.
- **Surface:** Solid panel backgrounds.
- **Elevated surface:** Glass-like or layered panels.
- **Primary text:** High contrast, usually white or off-white.
- **Secondary text:** Muted tones for balance.
- **Muted text:** Subtle hints and small labels.
- **Accent:** Sparing use of vivid colors for glowing effects or CTAs.
- **Borders:** Very subtle, low-opacity strokes.
- **Glow & Gradients:** Controlled, soft lighting effects to simulate depth.

## Typography
Bold, modern, highly readable, and visually hierarchical.
- **Display text:** Massive, editorial style.
- **H1, H2, H3:** Clear heading hierarchy.
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif.
- **Labels, Captions, Navigation:** Small, refined, often uppercase with tracking.

## Spacing & Layout
- **Maximum content width:** Constrained for ultra-wide monitors.
- **Grid & Columns:** Structured but capable of breaking the grid for editorial flair.
- **Section spacing:** Generous breathing room between content.
- **Hero composition:** Layered, deep, perspective-driven.
- **Card spacing:** Consistent internal padding.
- **Mobile spacing:** Adapted for touch.

## Surfaces
Use selectively:
- Rounded cards
- Semi-transparent surfaces
- Subtle borders
- Background blur
- Soft shadows
- Controlled glow
