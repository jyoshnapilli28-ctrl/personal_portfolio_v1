# 3D & Immersive Design

Create a 3D experience around the hero or another major visual section based on the PDF specification.

## Core Features
- Large-scale immersive 3D visual
- 360° interactive presentation
- Depth and Perspective

## Interactions
- Mouse movement & Touch movement
- Rotation
- Scroll-based translation
- Perspective changes

## Constraints
The 3D experience must:
- Feel premium and respond smoothly.
- Support the content (not block navigation or reduce readability).
- Not destroy performance.
- Provide a simplified fallback for mobile and low-powered devices.
